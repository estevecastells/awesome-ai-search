# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list useful. Pull requests are welcome, but inclusion is selective.

## What Belongs Here

Tools, open-source projects, first-party documentation, datasets and research directly related to measuring or improving visibility in AI-generated search and answer experiences.

## Inclusion Criteria

- The entry must have a working canonical URL and public evidence of usable functionality, such as documentation, pricing, a testable demo or an open-source repository.
- The capability described must be directly relevant to AI search, GEO or AEO. Generic SEO, writing, analytics and marketing tools do not qualify without a documented AI-search workflow.
- Descriptions must be verifiable from public documentation and avoid guarantees, rankings, unsupported metrics and marketing superlatives.
- Open-source projects must have an explicit license, installation documentation and recent activity.
- Vendor reports must state a publication date, sample size and methodology. They belong under Industry Reports, not Peer-Reviewed Research.
- Maintainer and contributor projects follow the same inclusion, wording and alphabetical-order rules.

## What We Decline

- Agencies, consulting services, waitlists and products without a publicly usable or documented surface.
- Affiliate pages, tracking links, reciprocal-link requests and paid placements.
- Competitor-comparison pages, launch announcements and thin promotional content.
- Duplicate entries or multiple homepage links for the same product. A separate API, dataset or open-source artifact may qualify when it has its own canonical documentation.
- Descriptions that cannot be verified, are materially out of date or overstate the public product.
- Mass-submitted directory entries that do not add distinct value to the list.

## Submission Format

- Add one entry per line in the most relevant section.
- Use `[Name](https://example.com) - Short, neutral, factual description.`
- Keep the description to one sentence and do not use em dashes.
- Use the canonical URL without affiliate or tracking parameters.
- Keep entries alphabetical within their section.
- Check for duplicates and broken links before submitting.
- Submit one suggestion per pull request when possible.

## Disclosure

State clearly in the pull request if you founded, built, work for, advise, invest in or otherwise represent the submitted project. Affiliation does not disqualify a useful resource, but undisclosed promotion weighs against inclusion.

## Review Process

Maintainers verify the linked resource, description, fit and distinct value. A working website alone is not enough for inclusion. Entries may be edited for accuracy, moved to a more appropriate section, deferred until public evidence improves or declined to keep the list focused.

## How to Add an Entry

1. Fork the repository.
2. Add the entry in the most relevant section using the format above.
3. Open a pull request with a clear title, the canonical public URL, evidence for the described capability and your affiliation disclosure.

Maintainers make the final call on scope and quality. Thank you.
